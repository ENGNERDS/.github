# Code of Conduct

## Standards

ENGINERDS is a small team of engineers who learn by building together. Everyone taking part in any of our repositories, discussions or issues is expected to be kind, patient and honest. Disagree with ideas, never with people. Give specific feedback rather than vague criticism, say what is good as well as what is wrong and assume good intent.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, abuse or personal attacks of any kind. Spam or off-topic promotion. Sharing anyone's private information without their consent. Posting secrets such as tokens, passwords and keys. Posting material that infringes someone else's copyright.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to any organisation owner listed on the [People tab](https://github.com/orgs/ENGNERDS/people). Reports are reviewed and appropriate action is taken, including removing content or access where necessary.
